# Alert me when a saved view has at least N rows

A saved view that you pinned to your home page shows a counter. Next to it a bell button lets you set a limit for that view.

1. Open the home page and press the bell next to a pinned view.
2. Enter the number of rows (1 to 100000) and press "Save alert". You can watch up to 20 views.
3. When you open the home page and the counter of the view is at or above your limit, you get one notification in the application. At most one notification a day per view.
4. To stop, press the bell again and choose "Remove alert".

The alert is personal. Only you can see it and it only notifies you. It counts what you may see on that screen, so it follows your permissions and your regions. It does not change any record or any permission.

The count is read in your browser when the home page loads, so the alert appears after you open the home page, not while you are signed out.
