# What-if on the site timeline

Above the bars of the site timeline, "What if" lets you try a date move without changing anything.

- Pick an open stage and a number of days (negative moves earlier). The stage and every open stage after it move by the same days, each keeping its own duration. A stage that already has an actual date stays where it is and stops the push.
- Dashed circles show where the moved stages would sit. The line under the controls gives the new finish date, the days it moves, the days after or before the baseline finish, and any stage that would end up dated before the one it waits for.
- It is a picture only. Nothing is saved, no date changes, and no task or gate is touched. Dates change only through the usual workflow.
