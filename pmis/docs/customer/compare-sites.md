# Compare two sites

Site page, tab "Compare". The page's site is the first; search a second site by code or name (only sites your regions cover are offered) and both are shown side by side. Read only.

- Status, current milestone, and the tasks that apply, approved, open and overdue, with the worst delay in calendar days against the planned date.
- Each stage with the days of both sites and the difference. The difference is the second site minus the first: a plus means the second took longer. Days are counted as in Time in stage.
- "Change site" picks another second site.

Nothing here changes any record.
