# Time in stage

Two places show how long stages take. Both are read only.

- Site page, tab "Stage times": for one site, each stage with its first start, finish, calendar days, planned days, days over plan and its rank (slowest first). The slowest stage is named at the top.
- Workload page, section "Slowest stages": every stage across the sites you can see, ranked by the average days of the finished stages, with the 85th percentile, the worst, and the open stages with their average age. Below it, the slowest site and stage pairs. The region filter of the page applies.

How the days are counted:
- A stage starts with the earliest actual start (or submission) of its tasks that apply, and finishes when every counted task that applies is approved. The finish is the latest approval date.
- Days are calendar days. An open stage counts up to today. A stage that has not started has no days.
- Planned days run from the earliest planned start to the latest planned date of the stage's tasks.

You see only the regions your view right on Sites covers. Nothing here changes any record.
