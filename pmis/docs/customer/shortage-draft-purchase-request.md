# Draft purchase request from a material shortage

In the Procurement and materials card of the dashboard, each shortage line (required more than dispatched) has a "Draft" button for users who may create records.

The button opens the new Procurement form with the site, the request type (PR) and a remark that names the item, the missing quantity, the required and the dispatched quantity. Nothing is created until the person submits the form, and the new record starts as an entry (a draft) that follows the normal approval. The person still chooses the scope and the order status.

The suggested quantity is the missing quantity. A reorder limit from the warehouse can feed it once that feature is in the product.
