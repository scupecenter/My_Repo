# Sub-tasks

A task or a register record can carry a short list of sub-tasks. Open the task or the record and choose the "Sub-tasks" tab.

- Each sub-task has a title, an optional owner, a status (open, in progress, done), a progress from 0 to 100 and an optional due date.
- Progress 100 means done. Ticking the box sets done. Moving the slider above 0 sets in progress.
- The top of the tab shows the roll-up: how many are done and the average progress. It is for information. It does not move the task or the record, its dates, its level or its approval.
- Who can see them: whoever can see the task or the record. Who can add, edit or remove: whoever has the entry level for that module in that region. The owner of a sub-task can always change its own status and progress.
- The owner must be someone who can see that region and module. Nobody gets new access from being named.
- Removing a sub-task hides it. It stays in the audit log. A record that has no region (a global record) takes no sub-tasks.
- A project data reset removes sub-tasks together with the task or record they belong to, and keeps the ones of kept records.
