# Availability on people chips

A small "Away: <kind>" chip beside the ID of any person who has an absence that covers today. It sits in the shared ID badge, so every people chip shows it (holders, task owners, groups, users).

- Source: the absence list the viewer may already read (own, the people they lead, all for a System Admin). Nobody sees more than the Absence screen shows. No new endpoint, no migration.
- Settings, Screens, Records has the switch "Show who is away on people chips". On by default, saved in this browser.
- English and Arabic. Informational only: nothing moves automatically.
