# On-Air celebration

A short celebration on the site screen when the site that is open reaches On-Air (the On-Air milestone turns done while the screen is open).

- Never plays on first load or when the site was already On-Air.
- Falling pieces plus a message; it ignores the pointer and closes by itself after a few seconds.
- Reduced motion (the operating system or Settings, Screens, Motion): the pieces are hidden and only the message shows.
- Settings, Screens, Motion has the switch "Celebrate when a site reaches On-Air". On by default, saved in this browser, like the motion choice.
- No new endpoint, no migration, no contract change. English and Arabic.
