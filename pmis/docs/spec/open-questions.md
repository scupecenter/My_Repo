# Open questions (P0)

These are only the real gaps found while writing the spec. Each one has a proposed default, which is used as a configurable setting until the owner decides.

The first three are carried over from CLAUDE.md "Still open":
- **S1. Documents for stages 4–10.** They are seeded as drafts (marked TBD in `task-catalogue.csv`), to be reviewed in the Document Library. *Default:* drafts are not mandatory until they are published.
- **S2. Real names per role.** They are entered after go-live. *Default:* synthetic users only.
- **S3. Final Aljeel brand.** *Default:* frappe-ui tokens in one file.
